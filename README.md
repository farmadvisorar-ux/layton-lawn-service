# Layton Lawn Service - Official Website

A website built for **Layton Lawn Service**, a locally owned and operated lawn care & property maintenance company with over 10 years of experience based in **Marshall, Texas (75672)**, serving **Marshall, Jefferson, Hallsville, Carthage**, and surrounding East Texas communities.

## ✨ Features

- **East Texas Branding & Trust Badges**: 10+ Years experience badge, fully insured, local Marshall 75672 roots, 5.0-star local reputation.
- **Interactive Lawn Care Cost Estimator**: Prospective clients can select service type (residential mowing, commercial grounds, flowerbeds & mulch, seasonal cleanups), property size, frequency, and city to receive an instant price range.
- **Interactive Service Area Checker**: Instant search tool for residents in Marshall (75672, 75670), Hallsville (75650), Jefferson (75657), Carthage (75633), Waskom, Elysian Fields, etc.
- **Interactive Before & After Transformation Slider**: Draggable visual slider showcasing overgrown turf vs. crisp, striped lawn and manicured flowerbeds.
- **Service Tabs with Visuals & Scope**:
  - Residential Lawn Maintenance (Mowing, edging, weed-eating, clean blowing, shrub pruning)
  - Commercial Property Grounds (Offices, shopping centers, churches, medical facilities, HOAs)
  - Landscape Enhancements (Hardwood mulch, pine straw, stone borders, weed barrier, shrub plantings)
  - Seasonal & Storm Cleanups (Spring scalping, fall leaf vacuuming, pine needle raking, storm branch clearing)
- **Local Reviews & Social Proof**: Testimonials from Marshall, Hallsville, Jefferson, and Carthage homeowners and commercial property managers.
- **Fast Free Estimate Form**: Interactive submission form with instant confirmation feedback and click-to-call direct lines.
- **Local SEO & Schema Markup**: JSON-LD `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` schema with geo-coordinates, operating hours, phone, and target zip codes.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Design**: Includes floating bottom action bar for one-tap calling and quote requests on smartphones.
